# Pocket Pals

Pocket Pals is a personal finance app concept that turns budgeting into a friendly, low-stress habit — a spending breakdown at a glance, upcoming payments, and playful savings goals.

- Designed the end-to-end experience in Figma, from user flows to a polished UI kit.
- Focused on reducing money anxiety: progressive disclosure of details, gentle nudges instead of alarms.
- Validated the goal-tracking concept through quick usability tests.
