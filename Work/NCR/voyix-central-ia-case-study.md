## Voyix Central: Information Architecture for a [New Back Office Platform](https://www.ncrvoyix.com/platform/restaurant-applications/operations)

**Problem & Goal**
Unifying the experience of 8 legacy products into one back office platform — a single pane of glass for Voyix Central. Capabilities span from Consumer Marketing, Reporting, Payments & Finance, and Online Ordering into a unified structure covering Menu, Inventory, Labor, and POS Configurations.

**Research Method & Process**
- Segregating capabilities by persona (Users).
- Validating capability scope with product managers (Context).
- Prioritizing legacy capabilities via card sorting with each persona (Content).
- Mapping and rearranging capabilities into correct modules (micro-frontends) through iterative research.

**Challenges**
| Challenge | What helped |
|---|---|
| AI-accelerated development | Good, collaborative team |
| Depth across 8 products | AI |

**Impact**
Actively designing the IA — directly informing platform development.

## Aloha Next: Mobile Research, Design & IA

Building the research foundation and IA for Aloha Next's mobile experience — recruiting participants through **Dscout** to run discovery research alongside ongoing design and IA work.

- Recruiting 5–7 customers across varied backgrounds for discovery research via Dscout.
- Running discovery interviews to surface mobile usability issues.
- Synthesizing findings into opportunities, shared with Pulse for feature prioritization.
- Building wireframes for rapid testing, iterating on customer and stakeholder feedback.

**Impact:** Continuously referenced in roadmap and planning discussions; findings actively shaping product decisions.

## Launch Research & AI-Assisted Synthesis Tooling

Running end-to-end research across the 2026 launch lifecycle while building the AI-assisted tooling that turns those findings into action faster — a continuous loop feeding back into every new launch.

- **Launch research** — Running research at each stage of the 2026 launch lifecycle with Sales and Product, delivering insights via an interim plugin and Dovetail reports.
- **AI-assisted synthesis** — Using GitHub Copilot + Figma MCP and a custom Figma plugin to aggregate and organize research insights faster.
- **Prioritization framework** — Ranking insights by customer impact, frequency, and business value.
- **Share & iterate** — Feeding learnings back to the team and into the next round of launch research.

**Impact:** Research continues to inform launch and readiness reviews, with an AI-assisted plugin actively speeding up synthesis under a shared prioritization framework.
