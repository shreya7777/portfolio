# NCR Voyix Case Study

## My Role

I'm a UX Researcher at NCR Voyix, a platform-powered leader in unified commerce for retail and restaurants. My work sits inside that platform: shaping the research, information architecture, and AI-assisted workflows behind products like Voyix Central, Aloha Next, and Pulse. This is ongoing work — a running log of what I'm building, not a single finished case study.

- $1.4T | Annual transaction volume | Processed through the unified commerce platform I help shape.
- #1 | POS & self-checkout | NCR Voyix is the top provider in the category.
- 140 | Years of innovation | A platform with deep industry roots, still being rebuilt for an AI-driven future.

## Voyix Central

Unifying the experience of 8 legacy products into one back office platform — a single pane of glass for Voyix Central. Capabilities span Consumer Marketing, Reporting, Payments & Finance, and Online Ordering into a unified structure covering Menu, Inventory, Labor, and POS Configurations.

- Segregating by Persona | Grouped capabilities by user persona to structure the IA around real usage.
- Validating Scope | Checked capability scope against product managers so nothing got lost in translation.
- Prioritizing via Card Sorting | Ran card sorting with each persona to prioritize legacy capabilities.
- Mapping to Modules | Mapped and rearranged capabilities into the correct micro-frontend modules through iterative research.

Two challenges shaped how this work got done: keeping pace with AI-accelerated development, and holding the depth of 8 different products in scope at once. A good, collaborative team — and AI itself — were what got it done.

**Impact:** Actively designing the IA — directly informing platform development.

## Aloha Next

Building the research foundation and IA for Aloha Next's mobile experience — recruiting participants through Dscout to run discovery research alongside ongoing design and IA work.

- Recruiting 5–7 customers across varied backgrounds for discovery research via Dscout
- Running discovery interviews to surface mobile usability issues
- Synthesizing findings into opportunities, shared with Pulse for feature prioritization
- Building wireframes for rapid testing, iterating on customer and stakeholder feedback

**Impact:** Continuously referenced in roadmap and planning discussions; findings actively shaping product decisions.

## Launch Research

Running end-to-end research across the 2026 launch lifecycle while building the AI-assisted tooling that turns those findings into action faster — a continuous loop feeding back into every new launch.

- Launch Research | Running research at each stage of the 2026 launch lifecycle with Sales and Product, delivering insights via an interim plugin and Dovetail reports.
- AI-Assisted Synthesis | Using GitHub Copilot + Figma MCP and a custom Figma plugin to aggregate and organize research insights faster.
- Prioritization Framework | Ranking insights by customer impact, frequency, and business value.
- Share & Iterate | Feeding learnings back to the team and into the next round of launch research.

**Impact:** Research continues to inform launch and readiness reviews, with an AI-assisted plugin actively speeding up synthesis under a shared prioritization framework.
