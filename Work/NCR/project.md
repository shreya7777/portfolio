### Focused work

## Back Office Management

Unifying the experience of 8 legacy products into one back office platform — a single pane of glass experience. Capabilities span Marketing, Reporting, Payments & Finance, and Online Ordering into a unified structure covering Menu, Inventory, Labor, and POS Configurations.

![A kitchen display screen I researched and designed for](Work/NCR/IMG_0924.jpeg)

**My work:**

- Segregating by Persona | Grouped capabilities by user persona to structure the IA around real usage.
- Validating Scope | Checked capability scope against product managers so nothing got lost in translation.
- Prioritizing via Card Sorting | Ran card sorting with each persona to prioritize legacy capabilities.
- Mapping to Modules | Mapped and rearranged capabilities into the correct micro-frontend modules through iterative research.
- Information Architecture | Developing the information architecture for a seamless back office experience customized fro each persona.

Two challenges shaped how this work got done: keeping pace with AI-accelerated development, and holding the depth of 8 different products in scope at once. 
A good, collaborative team — and AI itself — were what got it done.

**Impact:** Actively handing off the Information Architecture to developers for building the MVP — directly informing platform development.


## Launch Research

Running end-to-end research across the 2026 launch lifecycle while building the AI-assisted tooling that turns those findings into action faster — a continuous loop feeding back into every new launch.

**My work:**

- Launch Research | Running research at each stage of the 2026 launch lifecycle with Sales and Product, delivering insights via an interim plugin and Dovetail reports.
- AI-Assisted Synthesis | Using GitHub Copilot + Figma MCP and a custom Figma plugin to aggregate and organize research insights faster.
- Prioritization Framework | Ranking insights by customer impact, frequency, and business value.
- Share & Iterate | Feeding learnings back to the team and into the next round of launch research.

**Impact:** Research continues to inform launch and readiness reviews, with an AI-assisted plugin actively speeding up synthesis under a shared prioritization framework.
