# NCR Voyix — Restaurant Tech Research

As a UX Researcher at NCR Voyix in Atlanta, I plan and run user interviews, customer feedback groups, usability studies, quantitative research, and competitive analyses to improve experiences for restaurant operators and guests.

- Evaluate complex workflows through heuristic reviews and user testing, identifying friction points and opportunities to improve efficiency, clarity, and decision-making.
- Partner with product, design, marketing, and sales teams to translate research findings into actionable recommendations across three product lines.
- Build AI-assisted research and design workflows using Figma MCP, Atlassian MCP, agentic Figma plugins, and VS Code to organize insights by priority and turn findings into early design concepts.
