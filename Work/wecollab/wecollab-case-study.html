Simplifying Project-Based Learning

Shreya Pabalkar · UX Researcher & Designer · Indiana University Industry Project · Oct–Dec 2024

WeCollab connects students, instructors, and clients through a streamlined Project-Based Learning ecosystem — helping students find real-world projects, clients submit and track proposals, and instructors manage collaboration end to end.

Problem & Goal

Unstructured workflows were breaking real-world collaboration

"Stakeholders in PBL/SL settings are looking to align educational and business goals but they lack the ability to create a symbiotic experience because of unstructured workflows. Therefore, meaningful contributions are not made."

Environmental Analysis

Analyzing the environment around students, instructors, and clients surfaced where their needs overlapped — and where the market pressure was building.

Participants & Methods

Ran user interviews, task analysis, and a post-test evaluation with 9 students, 1 instructor, and 1 client — gathering expectations, pain points, and priorities, then watching them navigate the Client Project Portal and dashboard tools before prioritizing improvements for the final prototype.

Platform Architecture

How the platform works, end to end

1
Input
Students submit proposals; instructors approve matches and grade; clients provide project briefs.
2
Matching & Workflow
Centralized matching engine plus workflow tools — drive, calendar, chat, progress tracker, and more.
3
Backend & Analytics
Cloud/edge computing for scale, data intelligence on match and task metrics, and visualization dashboards.
4
Role-Based Output
Students get proposal & feedback dashboards; instructors get oversight tools; clients get deliverable tracking.

Testing Iteration 1 across all three roles

Student App
What WorkedAggregated docs, grading integration.
What Didn'tNo team spaces, no real-time feedback.
FixAdd profiles, team spaces, and rejection feedback.
Client View
What WorkedEasy proposals, showcase of past projects.
What Didn'tNo messaging or status updates.
FixAdd in-platform messaging and proposal tracking.
Insights Gathered

Desirability, feasibility, and viability

1
Desirability
Students valued the fairness of merit-based admission; clients valued detailed proposals; user-friendly workflows mattered most to both.
2
Technical Feasibility
Clients found the portal intuitive but wanted calendar integration and deeper communication; students noted a learning curve for workflows.
3
Business Viability
Clients saw value in matching but wanted tiered commitment options; instructors valued dual-layer approval for quality control.
