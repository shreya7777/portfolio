# Vosyn Case Study

## My Role

I was a UX Research Intern at Vosyn, an AI company building real-time voice synthesis and multilingual translation tools that break down language barriers for cross-cultural content — catering to 60+ languages globally through automation in voice models, with accessibility built in so the experience works for everyone. My focus was VosynVerse, a product that lets people experience translated media in their own language in real time. Coming in, the core translation and voice-synthesis tech already worked — my job was figuring out why that wasn't yet a complete, holistic content experience.

Research surfaced three gaps holding the product back:

- 20% | Low user engagement | Lacked interactive elements for engagement, retention, and community-building — the experience felt passive.
- 60% | High cognitive load | A confusing onboarding process with too many steps overwhelmed new users with a steep learning curve.
- 5% | Limited real-time adjustability | Minimal control over translation speed and language switching hurt comprehension.

## Research Process

I ran a multi-method research sprint aimed at four goals — understanding content interaction (solo and shared), improving onboarding, validating third-party and AI-bot integrations, and boosting community engagement — to make sure every design decision was grounded in evidence, not assumption.

- Literature Review | Reviewed prior research and current viewing trends to ground the work in what's already known.
- Market Benchmarking | Comparative analysis across 17 AI-based tools to see how competitors were solving similar problems.
- Heuristic Evaluation | Usability assessment of 20+ existing screens and 5 user flows to find friction before ever talking to a user.
- User Interviews | Gathered direct insights from 8 users, backed by think-aloud testing and task analysis to surface interaction bottlenecks.

## Reimagining the Experience

- Human Interaction | Designed the content interaction flow — share, comment, support, adjust real-time translation speed, and pull up multilingual transcripts. Benchmarked competitor engagement patterns, prototyped comment overlays and a speed slider, and validated with SUS surveys. Result: +30% feature engagement.
- Onboarding & Integrations | Redesigned onboarding and scoped a third-party app integration flow. Identified where users already overlapped with other OTT tools, worked through early stakeholder pushback with supporting data, and secured buy-in. Result: stronger platform interoperability.
- Landing Page Clarity | Rebuilt the landing page to cut the clicks needed to understand and access the platform, using task-based evaluations and design huddles to align the team on a minimalist, AI-first direction. Result: smoother onboarding, higher first-time satisfaction.
- Watch Parties & Beyond | Designed a watch-party feature so multiple people could view the same content in their own language at once, plus 5+ WCAG audits, an AI feedback bot spanning 60+ languages, and early IoT concepts — smart glasses and portable devices — for language learning on the go.

Design artifacts along the way:

- Built a detailed user persona grounded in real usage patterns
- Sketched 30+ low-fidelity wireframes and 20+ mid-fidelity screens across iteration cycles
- Developed high-fidelity prototypes to validate the final usability and engagement improvements

## Results

- +30% | Feature engagement | Increase in engagement and satisfaction through the new interactive flows.
- 25% | Usability improvement | Fewer unnecessary steps across key workflows.
- +15% | WCAG compliance | A more inclusive, accessible experience for every user.

- Reduced onboarding clicks and overall cognitive load
- Enabled real-time multilingual content sharing, turning solo viewing into a group experience
- Research insights helped shift VosynVerse's identity from a simple streaming tool to a global translation core

## Difficulties

- Limited Resources | Had to get creative to hit research goals within real constraints — a lesson in thinking outside the box and prioritizing ruthlessly.
- Fast-Paced Work | The internship's pace demanded a constant communication loop — regular check-ins and quick feedback cycles kept everyone aligned.
- Strategic UX | Learned to turn research and data into actionable design decisions that created real, measurable impact for users.

The biggest recurring challenge, though, was cultural — navigating language and cultural differences across a truly global user base, and a steady reminder of how much empathy and adaptability this kind of work requires.
